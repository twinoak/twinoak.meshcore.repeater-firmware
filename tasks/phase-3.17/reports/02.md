# p3.17-02 — a scope the host steps

Sessions 10 and 11 on the unit at Bellevue (25 November) showed that the far echoes of a wide
`scope` are the **sweep plugin's** and not the chip's: the receiver parked on 912.0 MHz never saw
the neighbour, a host-stepped `sweep` over the same place never saw it either, and an echo is gone
when a segment begins at its place and stands the settle. The plugin reads within some 10 µs of
writing a frequency and at certain frequencies the synthesizer needs 30 to 40
(`tasks/phase-3.17/FOLLOWUPS.md`, *Sessions 10 and 11*). Two of those four frequencies are round
and two fit no rule, so a list of places to cut at cannot be written down — and this task adds the
**other way to sweep** instead: **`scope … stepped`**, in which every point is tuned with the
chip's own command, stood on, and read once by the host. The plugin's way is untouched and is
still what a line with no word gets, to the byte of its `ack` and its arm.

The lines of the garden's new list, printed by this firmware's own `planScope()` and
`scopeAckMessage()` (`out/p3.17-02/ack-lines.txt`, built from `out/p3.17-02/ackdemo.cpp` in the
container that runs `[env:native]`):

```
scope 800 960 320 467 1000           n=320 … interval=1000ms sweep=4ms settle=500us
scope 800 960 320 467 1000 stepped   n=320 … interval=1000ms sweep=160ms settle=500us mode=stepped
scope 863 876 0 58.6 1000            n=222 … interval=1000ms sweep=7ms settle=500us
scope 863 876 0 58.6 1000 stepped    n=222 … interval=1000ms sweep=111ms settle=500us mode=stepped
```

**No board and no session.** Nothing on this machine can see an echo, so the whole of *What only
the unit can show* is below under *Not verified here*, and
`tools/noisescope/lists/garden-e.txt` is that session's program: the wide span both ways, twice
each, then 13 MHz at 58.6 kHz both ways.

## Deviations from the spec or the task

1. **The arm of a stepped segment is the plugin's arm, unchanged — so point 0 stands twice.**
   Deliverable 2 asks for the frequency through `ScopeArmRadio::setFrequency` and "the image
   calibrated for each 32 MHz range exactly as an arm does it today", and the arm is also the only
   thing that puts the receiver **in continuous receive at the plan's bandwidth** (the plugin's
   `setSweep()` does `setRxBandwidth`, the RF switch and `setRx(RX_TIMEOUT_INF)`;
   `vendor/sx126x_spectrum/SX126xSpectrumScanner.h` ~L131). So `SteppedScope::slice()` calls
   `armScopeSegment()` for a segment the cursor has just reached and nothing else, which ends with
   the plan's settle on the first point — and the first point then stands again as a point of its
   own. It costs one settle a segment (500 µs of a 32 ms slice) and keeps one arm in the firmware
   rather than two.
2. **A plan of one segment does not re-arm and does not stand before every sweep.** That is phase
   3.16's task 02 behaviour for a plugin scope (`serviceScope`'s second branch) and it exists
   because the plugin comes to each sweep from the span's last point. A stepped sweep stands on
   **every** point, so there is nothing that stand would add: a one-segment stepped plan arms once
   and steps for ever, which `ASteppedPointIsTheFrequencyThenTheStandThenOneReading` holds (no
   second `standby;`, no second `setSweep(`).
3. **`ScopeArmRadio` gained a pure virtual `readLevel()`, so `FakeArmRadio` of `test/test_scanner`
   gained one too, its log grew from 512 bytes to 16 kB, and its `settle()` now advances its
   clock.** A radio that cannot read a level cannot run a stepped sweep, so this one is not
   defaulted the way `settle()` was. The log had to grow because a stepped sweep of 320 points
   writes three calls a point, some 13 kB; and the fake's `settle()` advances `clock_us` because on
   the board that call **is** a delay (`ChipArmRadio::settle`), which is what makes a stepped
   frame's `us` the stands it actually made. No assertion of an existing test was weakened by
   either.
4. **`kScopeWordRefusal` now names `stepped`:** `refused: ldo or dcdc, boost or noboost, stepped -
   one word of each at most, anywhere after the two ends`. `stepped` is not one of a pair, but the
   same word twice says nothing new — phase 3.16's task 03's own rule — so it is refused in that
   sentence rather than silently ignored. The test that holds the sentence
   (`BothWordsOfAPairOnOneLineAreRefusedInASentence`) asserts substrings and needed no edit.
5. **The `err` of the ROM gate was reworded.** It said *nothing was uploaded - use sweep for a
   stepped spectrum instead*, which now names the new word for something it is not: a
   `scope … stepped` still goes through `beginPlugin()` and so still needs this ROM. It now ends
   *use sweep instead - `stepped` needs this ROM too* (153 characters of the 160 an `ack` holds).
   Teaching a stepped scope to run without the plugin would be a bring-up of its own and is not
   this task's.
6. **`ScopePlan::segment_ms` is now "the longest one pass of `loop()` is held" and not "the longest
   one plugin call holds the chip".** For a plugin scope it is the same number it always was; for a
   stepped one it is the slice. Nothing reads the field but the tests, and the two bounds those
   tests assert (RadioLib's 1000 ms wait for BUSY, and 50 ms for a slice) are each asserted for
   their own mode.
7. **The 40-character example list gained five lines and its test's comment changed.**
   `kWordLines` now carries `scope 780 960 0 467 1000 stepped` (32 characters, deliverable 1's
   line) and four more; `EveryExampleLineOfTheWordsFitsASessionsFortyCharactersOrIsSaidNotTo` said
   *nine of the ten fit* and now says *all but one*, which is still phase 3.16's own long example.
   A line with every number and all three kinds of word is past **two** bounds at once — twelve
   words where `kMaxTokens` is 11, and 56 characters where a session's step has 40 — and
   `TheWordSteppedIsTakenWhereverItStandsAndWhatIsLeftIsReadAsItAlwaysWas` says so outright: the
   longest line that still parses is `scope 780 960 0 467 1000 max 5.6 500 stepped ldo`.
8. **`AfterSuchAScopeTheRadioIsTheBuildsRegulatorAndTheGainBoostLeft` gained two assertions**, which
   is deliverable 6's *extended*: a plan nobody filled in is `kScopeModePlugin` (and `stop()` drops
   the plan, which that test already held), and `src/roles/scanner/Radio.cpp` branches on
   `g_scope.plan.mode` in exactly one place — so a mode cannot outlive the scope that asked for it.
9. **`tools/noisescope/scope_plans.json` gained no row.** The table holds the points, the segments,
   the step, the bandwidth, the first point, the width and the settle of a plan, and the mode
   changes none of them — `TheModeOfAScope.test_the_word_changes_no_point_no_segment_and_no_bandwidth_of_a_plan`
   holds every one of those fields and every segment equal between the two ways. A row would have
   held nothing new. The firmware's own test and `test_echoes` read the file unchanged: 22 rows, 0
   disagreeing.
10. **No new bullet in `docs/bench.md`.** `tools/bench/walk.py` holds that page's bullets to its
    walk one for one, so a new bullet is a new walk step and this session has no board to write one
    against (phase 3.16's task 02, deviation 9; phase 3.17's task 01, deviation 7). This task's
    verification is a garden session, which is below and in `garden-e.txt`.

## Decisions taken without asking

- **A stepped point's level is `GetRssiInst` over −2, and a reading that would be 255 is written as
  254.** The plugin files the RSSI register's own byte per point (`spectrum.asm`: *RADIO_BUF[i] =
  RSSI of point i, -2 \* dBm like GetRssiInst*), and RadioLib's `getRSSI(false)` is that register
  over −2 (7.8.0, `SX126x.cpp:641-654`), so the chip half multiplies it back. `kScopeNoReading` is
  255 and means *a point nothing was read for*, so a reading of −127.5 dBm is written as −127 dBm
  rather than as "no reading" — half a decibel, at a level no receiver of ours reports.
- **The slice is 50 ms.** A plugin call holds the chip for a whole segment, up to about 86 ms (250
  points at the narrowest bandwidth, the fork's own figure), and the loop comes round between two
  of them; a slice no longer than that keeps a stepped sweep's hold on the loop inside what a
  plugin scope already does, and 160 of them fit in the 8 s loop check. A point is never cut in
  half, so the worst case is two points of 20 ms.
- **The stand is `max(settle, (L + 2) updates)` and the latency table is written out in
  `Sweep.h`.** The task fixes the formula; `SX126xSpectrumScanner::latencyFor()` is **private** in
  the vendored class, so the three numbers are copied with the file, the function and the line
  cited beside them, as `docs/upstream.md`'s rule asks for a line of somebody else's code.
- **`us` and `usx` of a stepped frame are the points' own work and no arm**, timed with the seam's
  clock around each slice and added over the sweep. That keeps the member the same measurement it
  is for a plugin frame (which counts the plugin's calls and no arm), so `us` over the point count
  is a point's cost either way — which is what a reader of a capture compares.
- **`mode=` is named only for a stepped scope.** The alternative was `mode=plugin` on every `ack`,
  which would have moved the bytes of every line typed before this task for no reader's gain: a
  capture with no `mode=` is the plugin's way, and `echoes.py` reads it as that and not as unknown.
- **The `ack`'s order is `segw=`, `settle=`, `mode=`, `reg=`, `gain=`** — the new field after the
  settle and before phase 3.16's two words, so that each stays where its own task put it and a
  reader written for either still finds it.
- **The word twice is refused**, as above, and a word that is not `stepped` is read as a number, as
  every other word on the line is.
- **`garden-e` is six steps of 85 seconds**, the seconds every other garden list uses, and its
  narrow line leaves its points to the bandwidth (222 at 58.6 kHz) because deliverable 7 writes it
  as `scope 863 876 0 58.6 1000`. The two readings of each way are steps 1 to 4, so a pair is read
  twice and a neighbour's traffic cannot be mistaken for a measurement.
- **`plan_of_frame()`'s own mode stays the plugin's.** A frame carries no mode, so a capture with
  no `ack` to read one out of is read as the way every line typed before this task was swept — and
  `--steps` says out loud, per step, that the mode of a step with no `ack` is unknown.
- **`tools/bench/fakes.py`'s fake scanner `ack` was left as it is**, as phase 3.17's task 01 left
  it: nothing parses a mode out of it and changing it would be a change with no test behind it.

## Not verified here (and why)

Nothing on this machine can see an echo or a sweep rate: `tools/bench/bench.py status` was not run,
no port was opened, nothing was flashed and `bench restore` was not run. There was no board and no
NoiseScope session in the checkout.

* **One session of `garden-e` on the unit at Bellevue —
  `tools/noisescope/lists/garden-e.txt`.** After a release carrying this task and *Fetch a scanner
  image* on the unit:
  `python tools/noisescope/hunt.py --sessions tools/noisescope/lists/garden-e.txt`, paste the six
  lines into the form on the session's page, and an hour later *Download the lines* into
  `tools/noisescope/captures/garden/`, then
  `python tools/noisescope/echoes.py --steps <file> --carrier 869.618`. **What says it worked:** in
  the frames that hold the neighbour's signal, steps 1 and 3 (the plugin's way) show the four
  places — about 816, 845, 888 and 912 MHz — and steps 2 and 4 (stepped) show **none** of them. The
  pair `--steps` prints for each of those four combinations says *the mode … (plugin -> stepped)*
  and counts the places only one of them has; those counts are the reading to take. `dev` should be
  `0000` throughout.
* **What a stepped sweep of the wide span costs, in sweeps a second.** `sps` of steps 2 and 4
  against steps 1 and 3: the plugin makes 11 a second with five segments, and this firmware plans
  the stepped sweep of that line at 160 ms, so something near 5 or 6 is what the plan says to
  expect. A number far under that is a finding about the slice or about what a point really stands
  for, not about the echoes.
* **The narrow pair, steps 5 and 6** (`scope 863 876 0 58.6 1000`, both ways): one segment of 222
  points, where the raised floor around a strong signal reaches about 4.4 MHz either side
  (`tasks/phase-3.16/FOLLOWUPS.md`). Whether a stepped sweep changes *that* reading is the one
  thing those two steps are for, and nothing here can say it.
* **That the `ack` of every step says its mode.** Everything above is read out of the `ack` —
  `mode=stepped` on steps 2, 4 and 6 and no `mode=` on 1, 3 and 5 — so a download whose acks are
  missing cannot be read as pairs at all (`--steps` says so per step). The acks' bytes are held by
  a host test; whether meshmaster.dk's *Download the lines* carries them is still the first
  download's to say (`tasks/phase-3.16/reports/04.md`).
* **The image check of `docs/bench.md` §9 has still never been walked** (22 October). One carrier, a
  wide `scope`, one peak — the bench's half, and it needs the board, the generator and
  `"rf": "antenna"` in `bench.local.json`. This task adds nothing to it and nothing of it was run.
