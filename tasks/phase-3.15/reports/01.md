# p3.15-01 — a histogram that adds up

A `hist` line is now the histogram of the scan that was started for it, and the firmware proves
that to itself with a sum before it says the line. The sequence for one frequency moved out of
`src/roles/scanner/Radio.cpp` into `measureHistPoint()` in `src/roles/scanner/Sweep.cpp` —
host-portable, behind a `HistRadio` seam with one member per call of RadioLib's — so **the chip
of 21 November is driven by a native test**, and `Radio.cpp` is the chip half and nothing else.

**Was the cause found?** No: the door was closed without knowing which way it opened. Nobody has
a part to ask, so the sequence holds whichever the cause was — a time floor under every
*completed* status, the engine's status register put back to *none* before every start, the
result read once and only after the completion, and the bins added up before a line is written.
Two of those three would each have been enough on their own for the lines of 21 November; the
sum catches a chip for which none of them is.

## RadioLib 7.8.0, as read on disk (`.pio/libdeps/scanner_rak4630_single/RadioLib`, pin

  `setRx(RADIOLIB_SX126X_RX_TIMEOUT_INF)`, and then `SPIwriteStream(SET_SPECTR_SCAN_PARAMS)` with

* `SX126x::spectralScanAbort()` — `SX126x.cpp:1208`. One line: `SPIwriteRegister(REG_RSSI_AVG_WINDOW, 0x00)`.
  **The status register again untouched.**
* `SX126x::spectralScanGetStatus()` — `SX126x.cpp:1212`. Reads `REG_SPECTRAL_SCAN_STATUS`
  (`0x07CD`) and answers `RADIOLIB_ERR_NONE` for `0xFF` (*completed*) and
  `RADIOLIB_ERR_RANGING_TIMEOUT` for the other three values (*none* `0x00`, *ongoing* `0x0F`,
  *aborted* `0xF0`; `SX126x_registers.h:128-132`). So *completed-or-not* is all a caller sees,
  and a *completed* left over from the frequency before is indistinguishable from this scan's.
* `SX126x::spectralScanGetResult(results)` — `SX126x.cpp:1220`. A burst read of 66 bytes into 33
  counters, unconditionally: it reports nothing about whether the scan had finished.
* `examples/SX126x/SX126x_Spectrum_Scan_Frequency` polls the same way this firmware did — start,
  then `while(radio.spectralScanGetStatus() != RADIOLIB_ERR_NONE) delay(10);` with one `Serial`
  print between the two. That is what speaks against the reading, and it is in the follow-ups as
  Thomas's to file upstream if a session on the unit shows the register does keep *completed*
  across a start.

## The watchdogs

One `hist` step is one frequency, and `measureHistPoint()` is the whole of it. The worst case is
`kHistScansMax` × `histWaitUs(65535)` = 3 × 1 124 774 µs = **3.37 s**; three scans that each
complete on time are 3 × 537 ms = **1.6 s**. The loop watchdog's deadline is **8 s**
(`LOOP_WDT_TIMEOUT_MS`, `src/roles/scanner/config.h`) and the hardware watchdog's is **60 s**
(`WDT_TIMEOUT_S`), so neither is near; `Hist.EveryDurationComesFromTheOneSamplePeriod` asserts
the 3.37 s against the 8 s so that a later change to `kHistWaitSlackMs` or `kHistScansMax` which
broke it would be a red build. At the default 2 048 samples the worst case is 3 × 84 ms.

## Deviations from the spec or the task

* **`hist 869.618 869.618 25 65535` — the task's second bench line — is refused by this
  firmware,** and that is not something this task changed. `planSweep()` wants
  `f1 > f0` and a span of at least one step (`src/roles/scanner/Sweep.cpp`), so a `hist` is two
  points or more; `869.618 869.643` is refused too, because `(869.643 - 869.618) * 1000 / 25` is
  `0.9999999999990905` in double. The line in `docs/bench.md` §9 is therefore
  **`hist 869.618 869.668 25 65535`** — two frequencies one step apart, each line adding up to
  65 535, the first about 0.55 s after its `ack` — and it says in so many words that one
  frequency alone is not a `hist`. Nothing in the specification speaks to `hist`; `spec/` and the
  task do not disagree anywhere else.
* **The two new bench lines are walked rather than a person's.** The task asks for them "as lines
  in `docs/bench.md` §9 for a person with the board on USB, not run by this session". §9 is a
  `bench walk` section (`docs/bench.md` §0b), adding up 33 integers is something a script does
  better than an eye, and `tools/bench/test_walk.py` holds every bullet of a walked section
  against the walk's own table — so the two are steps `9.2a` and `9.2b` of `bench walk 9`, with
  a test that a scanner whose bins come to 15 is a *differs* naming what each line came to. **No
  port was opened by this session** (below).

## Decisions taken without asking

* **The wait is `2 × histScanUs() + kHistWaitSlackMs`** (50 ms), replacing `samples / 100 + 100`
  ms. The old number was 7× the scan at 2 048 samples and 1.4× it at 65 535, which is the wrong
  way round; the new one is proportional to the only thing that scales, and it is what bounds the
  3.37 s above. 84 ms at the default, 1.12 s at the most.
* **The status register is written back to *none* before every start, and not relied on.**
  `RADIOLIB_SX126X_REG_SPECTRAL_SCAN_STATUS` (`0x07CD`) through `Module::SPIwriteRegister()`,
  which is public at 7.8.0 — the same door `readDeviceErrors()` already uses for the scope. The
  task says to use it as well if it can be put back, and not to rest on it: whether the chip's
  own patch keeps a host's write of that register is not known, so the fake of the test of
  21 November is deliberately a chip that **ignores** the write and reads *completed* for ever.
  The floor is what saves that chip; the write is what would save a chip whose counters are
  slower than 8.2 µs a sample.
* **The status register is read raw instead of through `spectralScanGetStatus()`,** so that
  *ongoing* and *aborted* can be told from *completed*. The four values are named in
  `Sweep.h` and held equal to RadioLib's by four `static_assert`s in `Radio.cpp`.
* **A `standby()` or a `setFrequency()` the driver refuses now ends the pass by name**
  (`scan standby failed (…)`, `scan retune failed (…)`), where both return values were discarded
  before. This is the same family as the task's deliverable 1: a retune the chip refused leaves
  it on the frequency before, and a histogram of *that* adds up perfectly well and would be sent
  under the wrong `f`. Two new `err` messages, no change to the three the firmware already had.
* **The `err` words are in the portable half** (`histErrorMessage()`), not in `Radio.cpp`, so that
  a test holds every one of them. `Radio.cpp`'s `serviceHist()` is now 24 lines.
* **`kHistSamplesMin` and the clamps are unchanged.** 256 samples is 2.1 ms of scan, which is
  still a scan; nothing here slows a pass down (that is this phase's own third follow-up).
* **The `completed_early` flag is carried out of `measureHistPoint()` and goes nowhere else.** It
  is not in any line of the protocol — no field was added — and it exists so that a test can
  assert the chip of 21 November was in fact met. If Thomas wants the firmware to *say* it, that
  is a line of its own and a later task's.
