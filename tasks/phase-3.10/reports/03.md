## Deviations from the spec or the task

  places in the Walter firmware say *that* it has run `v0.1.2` since 8 November
  2026, and none says whether it went in over SWD from its own Walter or over
  the cable; the unit's own first update over LTE failed that day. The notes and
  README therefore say the fact and not a mechanism, and README says in so many

  Corrected from the Walter firmware's records (above). This is the second
  release in a row where a fact about the unit's radio lived only in the other
  repository; `v0.1.2`'s report said the same of 7 November. It is worth a
  standing follow-up and one is written below.

  `v0.1.2`, which is that line's subject, but **none of its three readings**

  written down anywhere, so there is nothing to tick — exactly the reason
  p3.9-03 left p3.8-02's line open. A parenthesis at the head of the line says
  so and points at this task's own line, which asks for the same three of

- **Nothing else.** `docs/release.md` and the task agree; `spec/` was not edited

## Decisions taken without asking

1. **The notes lead with the totals and give the schedule a section of its own,
   lower down.** The task asks for *what the totals are and why*, *what they are
   not*, and *that channels 1 to 5 are unchanged*; 01's change of schedule is
   none of those three, but it is the thing that makes a total honest and it
   changes two observable behaviours (a reading arrives 845 ms after a restart;
   a sweep can be a gap). So it is in the body, after the three the task names,
   written for the person who will watch a console rather than for the person
   who wants the arithmetic.
2. **The airtime and the byte counts are repeated as arithmetic, and labelled.**
   `+393 ms` is the most consequential number in this release for anybody with a
   duty-cycle budget. It was computed in 02 from the driver's own formula and it
   has not been measured, so the notes give the two figures and the report says
   where they come from; neither claims a measurement.
3. **The notes say the LTE path has both halves for the first time.**
   `v0.1.2`'s notes claimed it could be offered over LTE because the radio had a
   golden image, which was true of the radio and not of the download: no
   download of either box had ever sent the header meshmaster.dk requires, and
   the first one failed in two seconds the day after that release. Saying so is
   one sentence and it is what a person offering this release needs to know.
4. **One bench line, in §0, asking for four readings.** The three of p3.9-03
   plus `linkcli.py lpp` answering ninety bytes with channels 6 to 14 named —
   the one reading that is *this* release — and a pointer to §5's p3.10-02 line
   for what the totals then say. Walking it ticks the three stale lines beneath
   it, which is the only way those get closed now.
5. **No *Latest* claim is made before the release exists.** The notes file names
   `blob/v0.1.3/...` links, which is what every previous release's notes do and
   which resolve the moment the tag exists.
