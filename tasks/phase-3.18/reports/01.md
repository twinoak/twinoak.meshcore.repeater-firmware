# p3.18-01 — a scope at a fixed gain

The task's first step decides whether there is a task: **find, in what Semtech itself has
published, how an SX1261/2 is put into manual gain mode and how one of its gains is chosen**, and
*if nothing of Semtech's says how, stop: merge the report alone* — no register is written on a
guess (`tasks/phase-3.18/01-a-scope-at-a-fixed-gain.md`, *First: how the chip is told*;
`tasks/phase-3.18/README.md`, *Semtech's word or nothing*).

**Nothing of Semtech's says how.** The mode is named once, in one line of a calibration procedure
in the data sheet, and the way into it is in no part of that document, in no register of its
register map, in none of Semtech's published drivers for the part, in none of its published
examples — including its own spectral scan — and in none of its application notes for it. The one
API the same procedure names for the next step, `SetRssiCalibration`, is not a command the part
has. Semtech *does* publish manual gain control for another of its parts, the SX128x, as two named
registers; for the SX126x it publishes a gain **calibration** table and the two values of
`boost`/`noboost`, and nothing else.

So this report is merged alone. **No file under `src/`, `test/`, `tools/` or `docs/` changed**: the
diff of this branch against `main` is this one file. The word `gain<N>` is not on the `scope` line,
`held=` is not in an `ack`, `echoes.py` is as it was and there is no `garden-f.txt` — a word that
parsed and held nothing would be an `ack` line with no gain behind it, which is worse than no word.
What a session on the unit can measure **today**, with the firmware on `main` and no new register,
is under *Not verified here*.

## What Semtech says, and where

The data sheet read is **SX1261/2 Data Sheet, Rev 2.2, DS.SX1261-2.W.APP, December 2024, 118
pages** (read at Semtech's as the task says; nothing of it is copied into this repository, and
nothing of it is quoted here beyond the two phrases the finding turns on).

- **§6.1.6 *RSSI Calibration*, pages 41–42, is the whole of what names the mode.** It is a
  procedure for determining the Gain Tune and Gain Offset values *of a hardware implementation*,
  with an RF generator: (1) set the device in GFSK mode and the RF frequency; (2) *"Setup the
  device in manual gain mode (disable the AGC (Auto Gain Control))"*; (3) *"Use SetRssiCalibration
  API to set all tunes and offsets to 0"*; (4) loop through all the different gains, including LNA
  boost mode, reading the instantaneous RSSI against the generator's level; (5–7) take the common
  error as the global offset and the individual errors as the tunes, write them, and check. **Step
  2 is never explained** — not in that section, not anywhere else in the document — and step 3's
  `SetRssiCalibration` is not a command of this part (§13's command list has no such opcode; it is
  a published command on Semtech's later parts, the LR11x0 and the LR2021, and RadioLib implements
  it for those two and for neither SX126x).
- **Table 6-4 is the thirteen gains, and it is a calibration table.** `AgcGainTune_1_2` (0x8F5,
  two nibbles), `_3_4` (0x8F6), `_5_6` (0x8F7), `_7_8` (0x8F8), `_9_10` (0x8F9), `_11_12` (0x8FA),
  `_13` (0x8FB, bits 3:0) — so the chip's own numbering of its gains is **1 to 13** — beside
  `AgcRssiMeasCalH`/`L` (0x89C, 0x89D), `AgcGforstPowthr` (0x8B9) and `AgcSensiAdjust` (0x8AC bits
  7:2). Every entry is a *correction* belonging to a gain. **Nothing in the table selects a gain**,
  nothing gives a gain's dB, and nothing says which number is the most gain and which the least:
  the table gives the EVK's tune values for two bands (all zero for 868–915 MHz) and no order. The
  word `gain<N>` the task designed therefore has a numbering of the chip's own to take — 1 to 13 —
  and no published meaning for either end of it.
- **§9.6 (Table 9-3, page 62) and §12 (Table 12-1, page 70) are the only places a gain is
  written:** register **0x08AC**, *Rx power saving gain* **0x94** (the reset value) and *Rx boosted
  gain* **0x96**. That register is `boost`/`noboost`, and this firmware already writes it through
  `setRxBoostedGainMode()`. There is no third value, no mode bit and no AGC-enable register in the
  map; the AGC registers of Table 6-4 are not in Table 12-1 at all.
- **What a level would mean at a held gain, as far as it is published.** §6.1.6 is explicit that
  the reading is corrected by per-gain tunes plus a global offset — and that the per-gain tunes are
  **0x0 for the 868–915 MHz band**, so in our band no per-gain correction is applied even with the
  gain control running; only the global `AgcSensiAdjust` is not zero there (0x25). 0x08AC is one
  byte and `AgcSensiAdjust` is its bits 7:2, so the two documented values carry that offset inside
  them: `0x94 >> 2 == 0x25` and `0x96 >> 2 == 0x25`, the boost selection being what is left in bits
  1:0 (0b00 and 0b10). That is an inference from two documented values and a documented bit range,
  and it is as far as the document goes — **whether the chip compensates a reading for the gain it
  actually used, beyond those tunes, is not stated**, so the task's question *is it still corrected
  for the gain that is held* has no published answer. (Worth keeping for whoever writes the next
  task: for a 470–490 MHz board the same table's offset is 0x22, so RadioLib's two constants carry
  the 868–915 MHz calibration byte and not that band's. Ours is an 868 board.)
- **Nothing of a gain outlives a reset.** 0x08AC is not in retention memory; §9.6 gives the three
  writes (0x029F = 0x01, then the register's address into 0x02A0–0x02A1) that put it there for a
  warm start, which is also all Semtech's driver's retention list does. Our `beginPlugin()`
  hardware-resets the chip at the start of every scope and at every reload
  (`src/roles/scanner/Radio.cpp`), so a held gain would have to be applied there and again after
  every `reload()` — which is what the task's deliverable 3 asked for, and still right.
- **The mode of the receiver is the right one.** Semtech's own procedure is written for the **GFSK**
  receiver (step 1), and the GFSK receiver is what both ways of sweeping use here: the plugin's
  `begin()` is `beginFSK(freq, 4.8, 5.0, 234.3, …)`
  (`src/roles/scanner/vendor/sx126x_spectrum/SX126xSpectrumScanner.h`, L102) and a stepped sweep
  measures through the receiver that arm set up. So if a way in is ever found, the place it would be
  applied is where this task said it was.

## Where else it was looked for (all of Semtech's, read on 26 November 2026)

- **`Lora-net/sx126x_driver`** (Semtech's own SX126x driver), at `a10c5dfd`:
  `src/sx126x_regs.h:110–114` defines `SX126X_REG_RXGAIN 0x08AC` — *"RX Gain value (0x94: power
  saving, 0x96: rx boosted)"* — and **it is the only gain or AGC register in the file**;
  `src/sx126x.c:1255–1264` is `sx126x_cfg_rx_boosted()`, which writes 0x96 or 0x94 and nothing
  else; `:1442` adds that register to the retention list. No manual gain, no gain selection, no
  RSSI-calibration call.
- **`Lora-net/SWL2001`** (LoRa Basics Modem), at `ee93fdd9`:
  `lbm/smtc_radio_interface/radio_drivers/sx126x_driver/src/` is the same driver, line for line —
  one 0x08AC, two values.
- **`Lora-net/LoRaMac-node`**, at `cf779a46`: `src/radio/sx126x/sx126x.h:140–142`, the same register
  and the same comment; `SetRxBoosted` is the only gain call of that API.
- **`Lora-net/SWSD003`** (Semtech's SX126x application examples), at `08912a23`:
  `sx126x/apps/spectral_scan/main_spectral_scan.c` and its `README.md` — **Semtech's own spectral
  scan** is continuous receive plus `GetRssiInst` read `NB_SCAN` times a channel, with the gain
  control running and no register of it touched. If Semtech had a published way to hold the gain
  for a scan of the spectrum, that example is where it would be.
- **`Lora-net/SWDR005`** (Semtech's SX128x driver), at `3d57f5ea`: `src/sx128x_regs.h` names
  *"Manual Gain Control control register"* and *"Manual Gain Control value register"* for **that**
  part (0x0895 and its neighbour). Semtech documents manual gain where it has it; the silence for
  the SX126x is not an accident of naming.
- **An earlier revision of the same data sheet** (Rev 1.2, DS.SX1261-2.W.APP, June 2019) has no
  §6.1.6, no Table 6-4 and **not one mention of a gain control, an AGC or a manual gain**: the
  change list of Rev 2.2 says the RSSI-calibration section was added after it. The register 0x08AC
  of §9.6 is the only gain in that revision too. So the mode is named in the newest revision and in
  no older one, and there is no earlier text that explains it.
- **Semtech's document list for the SX1262** (the product page's design-support list, read 26
  November 2026): AN1200.28, .33, .35, .36, .37, .40, .48, .53, .54, .58, .59, .62, .64, .66, .73,
  .80, .81, .86, .87, .91, .94, .96, .115, the reference designs and the development-kit user
  guide. **Not one on gain, AGC or RSSI.** (.48 is CAD, .33 is crystal trimming, .37 is thermal

- **Semtech's developer forum, where the question was asked** (*"Is there Gain Control in LoRa

  `forum.lora-developers.semtech.com` has no DNS record, and

## What the third parties have (described, and not used)

Each of these is code or a document with **no word of Semtech's behind it**. By the task's rule
none is written on a guess; they are described and left for Thomas.

- **RadioLib 7.8.0** (the pin, `platformio.ini`): `src/modules/SX126x/SX126x_registers.h:120–122`
  has three values for 0x08AC — `0x96` boosted, `0x94` power saving, and **`0xCB`, commented
  *"spectral scan"***, which Semtech documents nowhere. `0xCB >> 2` is 0x32, which is neither band's
  `AgcSensiAdjust`, and its low bits are 0b11, which Table 9-3 does not have: writing it is exactly
  the guess the task forbids. `setRxBoostedGainMode()` (`SX126x_config.cpp:399–412`) is the only
  write of that register in the library; `resetAGC()` (`SX126x_commands.cpp:11–40`, declared
  `SX126x.h:191–196`) is a warm sleep, `Calibrate(ALL)`, an image calibration and the re-apply of
  DIO2 and boost — it **restarts** the gain control and holds no gain.
- **MeshCore at the pin `81dbae59`**: `src/helpers/radiolib/RadioLibWrappers.cpp:74` and
  `src/Dispatcher.cpp:134` — the repeater's own receiver resets the AGC that way
  (meshcore-dev/MeshCore PR #1743, *"fix agc reset on SX126x, SX1276 & LR11x0 chips"*, merged
  3 March 2026). Its author's own account is that on these parts the gain control can be reset and
  not chosen, and the data-sheet pages the PR cites are the calibration and sleep ones. A reset is
  not a hold — but it is evidence from the field that this AGC does get stuck after a strong signal,
  which is a cousin of what Bellevue shows.
- **ST's STM32WL sub-GHz driver** (`STMicroelectronics/stm32-mw-subghz-phy`, at `2d781a69`,
  `radio_driver/radio_driver.h:286–295`) names more AGC registers of the same die than Semtech
  documents — `SUBGHZ_AGCRSSICTL0R` 0x089B, `SUBGHZ_AGCCFG` 0x08A3, `SUBGHZ_AGCGFORSTCFGR` 0x08B8,
  `SUBGHZ_AGCGFORSTPOWTHR` 0x08B9 — and documents none of them as a manual-gain switch; its own
  driver writes only the GFO-reset threshold, with the value Table 6-4 gives.
- **A module vendor's note** that writing register 0x08B5 improves the AGC under strong
  interference circulates in forum posts and in other firmwares. No Semtech document behind it.

## Deviations from the spec or the task

1. **Deliverables 1 to 9 are not built.** That is the task's own first step and not a departure from
   it, but it is the shape of this pull request and belongs here: the word, the `ack`'s `held=`, the
   arm, the tool's column, the list and the words all wait on a published way to hold a gain.
2. **§6.1.6 is *RSSI Calibration*, not a gain-control section.** The task file reads it as *"a gain
   control with thirteen gains and a manual gain mode"*. In substance that holds — the chip has
   thirteen gains, numbered 1 to 13 by Table 6-4, and a manual gain mode is named — but the section
   describes **how to calibrate the RSSI of a board**, and the thirteen entries are thirteen
   *corrections*, one per gain, and not thirteen selectable settings. Nothing in Rev 2.2 says which
   number is the most gain, what a gain is worth in dB, or that a gain can be chosen by a host at
   all. A later task should start from that reading rather than from the task file's.
3. **`RUNBOOK.md` §5 would call this a stop condition** (*the design the task rests on turns out not
   to hold*) and would leave the branch unmerged with `tasks/phase-3.18/logs/STOPPED-01.md`. The
   task file says the opposite for exactly this case — *stop: merge the report alone* — and the
   phase README repeats it (*Semtech's word or nothing … or merges its report alone*). The more
   specific instruction was followed: the report is merged and **no `STOPPED-01.md` was written**,
   so the driver sees the task done and the phase has its one task. Nothing is left open that this
   report does not say.
4. **No line in `release-notes/unreleased/`.** Nobody with a radio can see anything in this pull
   request (`docs/release.md`, *The notes*).

   examples for the part (`SWSD003`); Semtech's application-note and user-guide list for the part;

   pair of phase 3.16's task 03, at the two narrow spans the rise fills. It needs no new code, it is

   It is written as lines in this report and **not** as `tools/noisescope/lists/garden-f.txt`: that

   different list under the same name would mislead the next session.
4. **`echoes.py --steps`'s rise was not built on its own.** It is the one deliverable that needs no

## Not verified here (and why)

No board in this session, and after this report nothing new to put on one. Two lists: what the unit
can say **now**, and what the first session has to show the day a way into the mode is published.

**Now, with the firmware on `main`** (v0.1.9's scanner, which carries phase 3.16's task 03).
`boost`/`noboost` is the one gain the chip's documented register gives us, and the `scope` line
already takes it for one scope. Four steps, in this order, each line 40 characters or fewer, each
read in the frames that hold the neighbour (-34 dBm) against the quiet frames of the same step:

```
scope 868.618 870.618 250 0 1000 boost      38   the 2 MHz picture, the gain every capture so far
scope 868.618 870.618 250 0 1000 noboost    40   the same at the power-saving gain
scope 863.118 876.118 250 0 1000 boost      38   the 13 MHz picture, boosted
scope 863.118 876.118 250 0 1000 noboost    40   the same at the power-saving gain
```

- **The rise at the boosted gain and at the power-saving gain**: how many of the 250 points stand
  10 dB or more over their quiet medians, in each of the two 2 MHz steps and in each of the two
  13 MHz steps. If `noboost` takes the rise away, register 0x08AC is the whole lever and nobody
  needs a manual mode; if the rise is the same in both, it is below the one bit we can reach and the
  question is Semtech's to answer.
- **The quiet floor of each of the four steps** (the median of the quiet frames), which says what
  the 3 dB of `boost` is worth here and gives the pair its baseline.
- **The neighbour's own reading in each** (the median of the seven points around 869.618 MHz in the
  lit frames), which says whether the strong signal itself reads the same at the two gains.

**The day Semtech's way in is known** — the session the task describes, unchanged and still the
proof:

- **The rise with the gain control on**, as the reference: of the 250 points of the 2 MHz picture
  and the 250 of the 13 MHz picture, how many stand over their quiet medians.
- **The rise at each held gain** — the most, two between, the least — the same count, the same span,
  the same frames.
- **The quiet floor at each held gain**: the median of the span in the quiet frames, which is what
  says whether a held gain keeps the floor where it is.
- **The neighbour's reading at each held gain**: the median of its channel in the lit frames, which
  says whether the gain that keeps the floor can still read a station at -34 dBm — and, with the
  gain control's own reading beside it, what a level at that gain is off by (there is no table for
  this board, and the firmware corrects nothing).
- **And whether the rise is the gain control at all.** That is the one thing no session on this
  machine can say.

Nothing was added to `docs/bench.md`: there is no new walk and no new line for the rig.
